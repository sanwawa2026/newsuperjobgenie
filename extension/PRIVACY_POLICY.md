# Privacy Policy for SuperJobGenie

**Effective Date:** September 29, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 29, 2026

SuperJobGenie ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices and demonstrates our compliance with Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies.

---

## 1. Single Purpose & Data Minimization
SuperJobGenie operates with a single purpose: to assist job seekers by comparing their resume qualifications against live job postings directly within supported job board websites. We collect and process the absolute minimum amount of data required to deliver this core functionality.

## 2. Information We Handle
- **Candidate Profile & Resume Data:** Any information you enter into the extension (e.g., job title, skills, years of experience, and uploaded resume text) is processed and stored **strictly on your local device** using `chrome.storage.local`.
- **Job Description Data:** When viewing a job posting on supported websites (e.g., Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor), the extension temporarily parses public job posting text to compute real-time match scores and skill alignments.

## 3. Data Storage & Transmission
- **100% Client-Side Storage:** All user data, resume text, and match history remain stored solely in your local browser sandbox.
- **No Third-Party Data Selling:** We do NOT sell, lease, monetize, or trade your personal data, resume information, or browsing history to data brokers, advertisers, recruiters, or any third parties.
- **No Remote Tracking or Telemetry:** SuperJobGenie does not include third-party tracking pixels, analytics beacons, or invasive tracking scripts.

## 4. Permissions Requested
- `storage`: Required to save your candidate profile locally so you do not have to re-enter your resume each time you open a job posting.
- `activeTab`: Required to inspect the currently active job board tab and display the floating HUD.
- `host_permissions`: Explicitly restricted to supported job board domains (e.g., `*.indeed.com`, `*.linkedin.com`, `*.glassdoor.com`, `*.greenhouse.io`, `*.lever.co`, `*.myworkdayjobs.com`). The extension does not and cannot access unrelated websites.

## 5. User Control & Data Deletion
You maintain complete control over your data at all times:
- You can clear your candidate profile and all stored resume text at any moment by clicking the **"🗑️ Clear"** button within the HUD.
- Uninstalling the extension from your Chrome browser immediately and permanently erases all associated local data.

## 6. Children's Privacy
SuperJobGenie is not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
