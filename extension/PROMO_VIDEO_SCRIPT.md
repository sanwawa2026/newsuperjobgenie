## 🛠️ 15-Minute Zero-Budget Production Guide

You don't need a professional video editing studio! Here is the exact stack top indie founders use to make viral Chrome extension videos:

1. **Screen Recording (Pick One)**:
   - **Screen Studio** (*Recommended, Mac*): Automatically creates buttery smooth camera zooms following your mouse clicks, rounded window frames, and 4K output.
   - **OBS Studio** or **QuickTime / Windows Game Bar** (*Free*): Record in 1080p 60fps at full browser window size (1920x1080).
2. **AI Voiceover (2 Minutes)**:
   - Go to [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) (Free plan available).
   - Paste the English script above, select a voice like **"Adam"** (tech narrator) or **"Rachel"** (crisp & friendly).
   - Download the generated `.mp3`.
3. **Background Music**:
   - Use YouTube Audio Library or Pixabay (search for "Tech Corporate Upbeat" or "Future Lo-Fi", 100% royalty free).
4. **Final Assembly (5 Minutes)**:
   - Drop the screen recording, voiceover, and music into **CapCut** (Free) or **iMovie**.
   - Sync the video cuts to the voiceover sentences.
   - Export as `1920x1080` MP4!
